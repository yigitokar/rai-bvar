We claim no new sampler: each block follows published work, credited where it is used, and what
was derived in our design notes is marked as such.
The full pseudocode is in \cref{app:algorithms}.
We write $\lambda^{\mathrm{eff}}_{i,t} = e^{h_{i,t}}o_{i,t}^2\tau_{i,t}$ for the effective variance of
structural equation $i$ ($\lambda_i o_{i,t}^2\tau_{i,t}$ under constant volatility); sums over $t$ run over
observed rows (padded rows have weight zero).

\subsection{One sweep}\label{sec:samp-sweep}

In structural form each equation, under \code{vmap}, draws $(\delta_i, a_i)$ jointly from one Gaussian
conditional, recomputes its residual and runs its volatility block atomically
(\cref{alg:sweep}).
In that block the transitory steps ($o \to p \to \nu \to \tau$) run before the persistent core, in
which the SV indicators are drawn just before $h$. % [V] tests/mcmc/blocks/test_robust.py::test_sample_eq_order_and_data_flow_spy (o → p → ν → τ → λ, Constant core); SV-core order: [V] src/rai_bvar/components/vol/sv.py (module docstring "One update"; SVCore.sample; read in code, no order test)
In reduced form a pre-phase step draws $\Pi \mid A, \lambda$ (\cref{sec:samp-cccm}), then each
equation draws $a_j \mid \Pi, \lambda_j$.
The coefficient steps condition on the exact Gaussian likelihood given $\lambda^{\mathrm{eff}}$ and are
marginal of the indicators, as \citet{delnegro2015time} require; the transitory steps with $\tau$
integrated out are partially collapsed Gibbs blocks \citep{vandyk2008partially}. % "whose trimmed draw is never conditioned on before it is redrawn" deleted here: duplicated in Appendix C (paragraph after tab:C-transitory)

\begin{algorithm}[hbp]% no [t]: a top float could land above the section heading, in §4
  \caption{One Gibbs sweep of one chain (compact form).}
  \label{alg:sweep}
  \small
  \begin{algorithmic}[1]
    \Require chain key, absolute sweep index $it$ (unsigned 32-bit), state
    \State \textbf{if} reduced form \textbf{then} $\Pi \gets$ CCCM scan over $\pi_j \mid \pi_{-j}, A, \lambda^{\mathrm{eff}}$
      \Comment{\cref{sec:samp-cccm}}
    \ForAll{equations $i$ \textbf{in parallel} (\code{vmap}); keys \code{fold\_in}($it$, block, $i$, sub-draw)}
      \State structural: $(\delta_i, a_i) \sim N(K_i^{-1}b_i, K_i^{-1})$; \ reduced: $a_i \mid \Pi, \lambda^{\mathrm{eff}}_{i,\cdot}$ by QR
        \Comment{\cref{sec:samp-coef}}
      \State $e_{i,t} \gets$ structural residual; \ $o_i \to p_i \to \nu_i \to \tau_i$ given $e_i$ and the persistent variance
        \Comment{\cref{sec:samp-transitory}}
      \State on $\log\tilde e_{i,t}^2 = \log e_{i,t}^2 - 2\log o_{i,t} - \log\tau_{i,t}$: Constant $\lambda_i \sim \mathrm{IG}$
        \eqref{eq:constant-core}; SV $s_i \to h_i \to \theta^{\mathrm{SV}}_i$ (\cref{alg:C-sv})
    \EndFor
    \State keep the first failure $(\text{code}, it)$, $\text{code} = 1 + 1000\cdot\text{block} + \text{equation}$
  \end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{The structural coefficient draw}\label{sec:samp-coef}

In the $\delta$-coordinates of \cref{sec:framework}, every equation regresses on one shared
design $F = [X \mid XM_{<n} - Y_{<n}]$; the inactive slots $a_{ij}$, $j \ge i$, are padded so that
the active draw stays exact (\cref{alg:C-structural}). % [V] tests/mcmc/unit/test_linalg.py::test_padding_is_exact; how they are padded ("prior mean 0, precision 1, zero columns, zeroed after the draw") moved to App. C (app:coef-paths)
With the weighted Gram $F^\top\Lambda_i^{-1}F$, $\Lambda_i = \mathrm{diag}(\lambda^{\mathrm{eff}}_{i,\cdot})$
in the precision $K$ (\cref{app:coef-paths}), the draw is a Jacobi-scaled Cholesky precision draw, % [V] src/rai_bvar/engines/mcmc/linalg.py (weighted_gram); the packed-Gram sentence and its full tag moved to App. C (app:coef-paths)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:precision-draw}
  \theta_i = s \odot L^{-\top}\bigl(L^{-1}(s \odot b) + z\bigr), \qquad
  LL^\top = \mathrm{diag}(s)\,K\,\mathrm{diag}(s), \qquad s = \mathrm{diag}(K)^{-1/2},
\end{equation}
with $z \sim N(0, I)$, so $\theta_i \sim N(K^{-1}b, K^{-1})$ exactly. % [V] tests/mcmc/unit/test_linalg.py::test_precision_draw_matches_dense_oracle, ::test_jacobi_scaling_with_large_precision_disparities
Under constant volatility without a transitory scale a deterministic rule picks one of three exact
fast paths, \code{eigen}, \code{fixed\_factor} or \code{cholesky\_const} (\cref{app:coef-paths}). % [V] src/rai_bvar/components/mean/linear.py (module docstring, four static paths); src/rai_bvar/engines/mcmc/resolve.py (resolve_const_path); per-mode detail moved to App. C (app:coef-paths)
The paths share the target; their tests need a conditioning-aware tolerance
(\cref{sec:validation}). % [V] tests/mcmc/blocks/test_linear_fastpaths.py::test_paths_agree_well_conditioned, ::test_paths_ks; tests/mcmc/unit/test_linalg.py::test_constant_paths_on_level_data_vs_45_digits
Where $a_i$ is drawn on its own, as in the reduced form, a Householder QR of the whitened design
gives the same draw as \eqref{eq:precision-draw} for the same $z$ without forming the precision
(\cref{alg:C-qr});
\cref{sec:robustness} shows why. % [V] tests/mcmc/blocks/test_contemp_lstsq.py::test_lstsq_draw_equals_the_cholesky_draw, ::test_extreme_scale_state. \subsection{The corrected reduced-form step}\label{sec:samp-cccm}

The columns $\pi_j$ of $\Pi$ are drawn from their exact full conditionals \citep{carriero2022corrigendum}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cccm}
  K_j = V_j^{-1} + X^\top \mathrm{diag}(W_{\cdot j})\,X, \qquad
  b_j = V_j^{-1}m_j + X^\top\bigl(g_{\cdot j} + W_{\cdot j} \odot X\pi_j\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $W_{tj} = (\Sigma_t^{-1})_{jj} = \sum_{i \ge j} A_{ij}^2/\lambda^{\mathrm{eff}}_{i,t}$ (with
$A_{jj} = 1$) and $g_{tj} = (\Sigma_t^{-1}u_t)_j$, with $u_t$ the current reduced-form residual, so $A$
is never inverted.
% Deleted here as duplicated in alg:C-cccm (Appendix C: "for all j in parallel; K_j does not depend on Pi"): "K_j does not depend on Pi, so by default all n precisions are factored in parallel before the sequential scan"; the default and its memory bound are in App. C (app:coef-paths).
The scan is block Gauss--Seidel on the joint precision of $\operatorname{vec}\Pi$
\citep{fox2017accelerated}. % [V] src/rai_bvar/components/mean/linear_reduced.py; tests/mcmc/blocks/test_cccm.py::test_random_problems_vs_sur_oracle; the memory-bound clause and its resolve.py tag moved to App. C (app:coef-paths)

(\citealp{bognanni2022comment}; \cref{app:ccm-flat}); it survives as mutant M1
(\cref{sec:validation-mutation}). % [V] tests/mcmc/blocks/test_cccm.py::test_ccm2019_regression_guard; tests/mutation/mutants.py (M1). \subsection{The stochastic-volatility core}\label{sec:samp-sv}

The SV core (\cref{alg:C-sv}) works on $y^*_t = \log(\tilde e_t^2 + c)$ with, by default, the relative offset
$c = 10^{-12}\psi$ (an absolute offset can be set). % [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/sv_arrays.py (C_REL, D-2); src/rai_bvar/components/specs.py (SV.offset="relative")
The $\log\chi^2_1$ density is approximated by the ten-component mixture of \citet{omori2007stochastic},
with component means $m_j$ and variances $v_j$ (the seven-component mixture of \citet{kim1998stochastic}
is an option), and the indicators are drawn by inverse CDF. % [V] src/rai_bvar/components/vol/mixture.py; tests/mcmc/blocks/test_mixture.py::test_indicator_draw_chi2
The path $(h_0, \dots, h_T)$ is drawn in one block from its tridiagonal Gaussian conditional
\citep{rue2001fast,chan2009efficient,mccausland2011simulation}, the ``all without a loop'' (AWOL)
sampler in the terminology of \citet{kastner2014ancillarity}, in non-centred scaled form
$\tilde Q = \sigma^2 Q$.
By default (\code{exact=True}) an exact Metropolis--Hastings correction removes the mixture
approximation: with $g$ the mixture density and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:w}
  w(h) = \sum_t \bigl[\log N(\tilde e_t; 0, e^{h_t}) - \log g(y^*_t - h_t)\bigr],
\end{equation}
the path proposal is accepted with probability $\min\{1, e^{w(h') - w(h)}\}$
\citep[Algorithm~3]{delnegro2015time}, an idea with a precedent in
\citet{stroud2003nonlinear}.\todo{N01 marks \citet{stroud2003nonlinear} ``not opened'': confirm its eq.~(11), or drop the citation.}\todo{N01 read Del Negro--Primiceri as NY Fed Staff Report 619 (rev.\ Oct.\ 2014) with its online appendix (App.~B.2, eq.~7); confirm that ``Algorithm~3'' is numbered so in the cited RES corrigendum, or cite the staff report.}
The chain is then exact for any mixture table and offset $c \ge 0$, which the validation exploits with a
deliberately coarse mixture (\cref{sec:validation}). % [V] tests/mcmc/blocks/test_sv_core.py::test_exact_h_log_accept_identity, ::test_block_geweke_exact_mode_with_coarse_mixture
The centred steps need no correction (\cref{app:exact-sv}). % [V] test_sv_core.py::test_centred_gamma_phi_log_ratio_identity, ::test_sigma2_log_ratio_identity_and_ig_gibbs. The reason ("they do not move h, and the indicator conditional does not involve the parameters") is deleted here: duplicated in Appendix B.3 (app:exact-sv)
The non-centred step (on by default, \code{asis=True}) interweaves
\citep{yu2011center,kastner2014ancillarity}; in exact mode it is
accepted or rejected once, with a ratio combining the auxiliary ratio and the change in $w$, a
combination derived in the design notes (\cref{app:exact-sv}) and not claimed as new.\todo{TODO 11: prior-art search for this combined ratio (docs/phase2/01\_omori\_mixture.md §10.3; \cref{app:exact-sv}).} % [V] test_sv_core.py::test_nc_mu_sigma_log_accept_identity, ::test_nc_accept_uses_w_of_the_post_h_step_state
A negative $\sigma'$ is folded together with $\tilde h$, which preserves the non-centred posterior by the
sign symmetry used by \citet{fruhwirth2010stochastic}; the cold start is described after
\cref{alg:C-sv}. % [V] test_sv_core.py::test_fold_is_measure_preserving_map, ::test_block_geweke_catches_abs_sigma_without_flip; the cold-start sentence and its [R]/[V] tags moved to App. C (after alg:C-sv)

\subsection{The random-walk path as a path Laplacian}\label{sec:samp-rw}

For $h_t = h_{t-1} + \sigma\eta_t$, $h_0 \sim N(m_0, V_0)$, the scaled precision is
$\tilde Q = \sigma^2 Q = \mathrm{Lap}_w + \mathrm{diag}(\delta)$: a path-graph Laplacian with unit edge
weights $w_t$ (zero after the last live state) plus $\delta_0 = \sigma^2/V_0$ and
$\delta_t = \sigma^2/v_{s_t}$.
The sampler (\cref{alg:C-laplacian}) carries each pivot as its excess $q_t$ over the next edge:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:excess-pivot}
  q_0 = \delta_0, \quad
  q_t = \delta_t + \frac{w_{t-1}\,q_{t-1}}{w_{t-1} + q_{t-1}}, \quad
  p_t = q_t + w_t, \quad
  \log|\tilde Q| = \sum_t \bigl[\log w_t + \operatorname{log1p}(q_t/w_t)\bigr],
\end{equation}
with $\log q_t$ where no next edge exists.
The $p_t$ are the Cholesky pivots of $\tilde Q$ (\cref{app:pivots}), but every term is
non-negative, so nothing cancels; assembling $\tilde Q$ first rounds away each $\sigma^2/v_{s_t}$ below
the spacing of its diagonal, and the level-direction pivot becomes rounding noise
(\cref{sec:robustness}). % [V] src/rai_bvar/engines/mcmc/linalg.py (laplacian_precision_sample); test_sv_core.py::test_awol_rw_laplacian_is_the_rw_system, ::test_rw_awol_at_a_tiny_sigma (60-digit reference; Apple M5 Max; Linux re-run is TODO 23)
We read the recursion as GTH-type subtraction-free elimination
\citep{grassmann1985regenerative,alfa2002accurate,ye2008computing}; we claim its application, not the
algorithm.\todo{TODO 11: confirm this positioning against GTH, Alfa--Xue--Ye, Ye (2008) and GMRF samplers.}

\subsection{Transitory components}\label{sec:samp-transitory}

$\log\varpi_k - \log k - z_t^2/(2k^2)$, with $z_t^2 = e_t^2/\lambda^{\mathrm{pers}}_t$, $\varpi_1 = 1 - p$ and
$\varpi_{k > 1} = p/(K - 1)$ \citep{carriero2024addressing,stock2016core}, and $p \mid o$ is beta

\subsection{The constant-volatility core}\label{sec:samp-constant}

Under constant volatility
\begin{equation}\label{eq:constant-core}
  \lambda_i \mid \cdot \sim \mathrm{IG}\Bigl(c_i + \tfrac{T}{2} + \tfrac{n_{\mathrm{active}}}{2},\;
  C_i + \tfrac12\textstyle\sum_t \tilde e_{i,t}^2 + \mathrm{quad}\Bigr), % [V] src/rai_bvar/components/vol/constant.py; tests/mcmc/blocks/test_constant.py::test_conditional_formula_masks_exact_zeros_and_coupling, ::test_draws_are_inverse_gamma
\end{equation}
where $(n_{\mathrm{active}}, \mathrm{quad})$, the number of coefficients whose prior scales with
$\lambda_i$ and half their prior quadratic form, is passed from the coefficient step of the same
equation: zero in Mode~I, all $k + \iota - 1$ coefficients in Mode~II, and the $\iota - 1$ contemporaneous
ones under \code{iw\_equivalent} ($\iota$ the one-based equation index).
This is the only interaction between the prior and the volatility block that the plan allows (for
the SOC/SUR dummy rows in Mode~II see \cref{sec:robustness-dummies}). % [V] tests/mcmc/runner/test_plan.py::test_coupling_without_coupled_core_is_rejected
