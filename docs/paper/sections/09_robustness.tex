\subsection{F1: a Geweke chain in the tail of the test prior}\label{sec:robustness-f1}

The weekly G-full Geweke test \citep{geweke2004getting} of \code{reduced_sv} failed on chain 3214 of 4000 at sweep 194 with status 1003 (block \code{EQ_CONTEMP}, equation~2), with equation~0 at log-variances $h \approx 31$--$37$ and $|y| \approx 2\times10^{8}$. % [R] commit 3d3f51b; tests/mcmc/blocks/test_contemp_lstsq.py l.4 (2e8, not the 1e8 of NOTES 10)
The precision of equation~2's contemporaneous coefficients, $K_a = V_a^{-1} + Z^\top W Z$ (regressors $Z$, inverse variances $W$), had exact eigenvalues 14.8 and $1.9\times10^{17}$; forming $Z^\top W Z$ lost the small one and the Cholesky pivot was zero, although the conditional is benign, with standard deviations 0.20 and 0.16. % [R] commit 3d3f51b; NOTES 10 (60-digit arithmetic; cond 1.3e16)
Rather than narrow the test prior, which would have hidden a real float64 weakness, the fix draws $a$ from a Householder QR factorisation of the whitened stacked design (\cref{sec:samp-coef,app:pivots}): its $R$ is the upper Cholesky factor of $K_a$, so the draw is the same function of the noise, but $K_a$ is never formed and the condition number is $\operatorname{cond}(K_a)^{1/2}$, not its square, the textbook case for QR over the normal equations \citep{higham2002accuracy,bjorck1996numerical}. % [R] narrowing rejected: commit 3d3f51b; [V] src/rai_bvar/engines/mcmc/linalg.py::lstsq_draw (docstring: "the condition number of A (√cond(K)) is never squared"); QR formula: app:pivots eq:qr-draw

\subsection[F2: the random-walk SV draw at a tiny sigma]{F2: the random-walk SV draw at $\sigma \approx 5\times10^{-9}$}\label{sec:robustness-f2}

The first full Clark--Ravazzolo attempt (\cref{tab:TabF1}) aborted with status 2001 (block \code{EQ_VOL}, equation~0) in an SV(rw) reduced-form fit at origin 1987Q2, burn-in sweep 318, with GDP's volatility scale at $\sigma = 4.98\times10^{-9}$. % [R] commit f60d736; NOTES 17 (ordering GDP, TBILL, INFL, UR; chain 0)
The state is valid: the non-centred prior $\sigma \sim N(0, B_\sigma)$, equivalently $\sigma^2 \sim B_\sigma\chi^2_1$, has positive density at $\sigma = 0$, and the same sweep's non-centred move would have returned $\sigma$ to 0.031. % [R] commit f60d736
In the scaled system $\tilde Q = \mathrm{Lap}_w + \operatorname{diag}(\delta)$ of \cref{sec:samp-rw}, the terms $\delta_t = \sigma^2/v_{s_t} \approx 10^{-17}$ fell below the floating-point spacing at the Laplacian's diagonal entries (2), so the assembled matrix was the singular path Laplacian and the level pivot, $\approx \sigma^2(1/V_0 + \sum_t 1/v_{s_t}) \approx 10^{-15}$, came out zero. % [R] commit f60d736 ("sigma^2 D ~ 1e-17", D = 1/v at observed t, test_sv_core.py l.305); DESIGN §3.7 (RW path)
The excess-pivot recursion of \cref{sec:samp-rw}, which we read as an application of known subtraction-free elimination, not a new algorithm, factors the same matrix without assembling it, with non-negative terms only, so nothing cancels. % [V] src/rai_bvar/engines/mcmc/linalg.py::laplacian_precision_sample; tests/mcmc/blocks/test_sv_core.py::test_awol_rw_laplacian_is_the_rw_system

\subsection{SOC/SUR dummy rows on the MCMC engine}\label{sec:robustness-dummies}

In Mode~II the dummy rows are weighted by $1/\lambda_i$, so the $\lambda_i$ step must count them; a hand-injected omission, recorded in the repository notes but not committed as a test, was caught by the analytic twin (\cref{sec:validation-twins}) but not by the dense oracles of the coefficient step. % [R] NOTES 12 (the injection is not committed, so how many quantiles it moved is not quoted)
For exact Geweke and SBC references, $\lambda_i \mid d$ is inverse gamma under Mode~II, and under \code{iw_equivalent}, where it is not, the base prior $\mathrm{IG}(c, C)$ is an exact rejection envelope (\cref{prop:envelope}). % [V] NOTES 13; src/rai_bvar/validation/dummies.py docstring (q_inf = GLS residual); tests/validation/test_reference_dummies.py::test_mode2_log_density_and_sampler
The $\lambda$ draws pass a Kolmogorov--Smirnov test against quadrature, and row 205 passes Geweke and SBC (\cref{app:dummies}). % KS: [V] tests/validation/test_reference_dummies.py::test_iw_equivalent_rejection_sampler. Row 205 statistics (G-small max|z| 2.24, G-full 2.76, SBC min p 0.018; stated in app:dummies): [R] NOTES 13; [REL] G-full 2.76 (stage 1) and SBC min p 0.0178 for row 205 in record/geweke.jsonl and record/sbc.jsonl at 7f880e1 (G-small 2.24: 62d15f6 only; row 205 passes G-small in CI run 40061724239); row 205 = structural_iw_equivalent_dummies (src/rai_bvar/validation/joint.py)

\subsection{Notes for other implementations}\label{sec:robustness-notes}

These generic checks name no package.
Implementations that (i)~form a weighted Gram for a Gaussian conditional draw should test regressors whose scales differ by many orders of magnitude (F1); (ii)~solve the random-walk SV system in scaled form should test $\sigma \to 0$ (F2); (iii)~fold a non-centred sign should flip $(\sigma, \tilde h)$ jointly, the map that leaves the posterior invariant \citep{fruhwirth2010stochastic}, not $\sigma$ alone (mutant M6, \cref{sec:validation-mutation}); and (iv)~score multi-step forecasts conditionally on simulated paths should inspect the Kish effective number of weights $\mathrm{ESS}_{\mathrm{K}}$ for tail outcomes (\cref{sec:eval-tail}).
