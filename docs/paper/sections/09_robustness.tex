\subsection{The pattern}\label{sec:robustness-pattern}

Three failures surfaced in the Phase~2 validation and replication runs, none from a user report: two float64 failures, F1 and F2, at states that are legitimate under the model, found by a Geweke test with a heavy-tailed test prior and by a replication of 24 orderings at 106 origins, and F3, a bug in the failure-reporting path. % [R] dossier 05 §2.1, §2.6; issue #3 (item 7); benchmarks/clark_ravazzolo.py at 8a622c5 (config "full": ordering_step 1, 2 x 25 x 106 ordering-stage fits)
In F1 and F2 the information was lost when the matrix was assembled, not when it was factored, so both fixes factor the data rather than the assembled matrix.
Because failures are values and are never jittered (\cref{sec:arch-mcmc}), both were reported rather than masked; \cref{tab:TabE1} summarises F1--F3. % [V] tests/mcmc/runner/test_failures.py; DESIGN §5.7 (D-18)

\subsection{F1: a Geweke chain in the tail of the test prior}\label{sec:robustness-f1}

The weekly G-full Geweke test \citep{geweke2004getting} of \code{reduced_sv} failed on chain 3214 of 4000 at sweep 194 with status 1003 (block \code{EQ_CONTEMP}, equation~2), with equation~0 at log-variances $h \approx 31$--$37$ and $|y| \approx 2\times10^{8}$. % [R] commit 3d3f51b; tests/mcmc/blocks/test_contemp_lstsq.py l.4 (2e8, not the 1e8 of NOTES 10)
The contemporaneous precision of equation~2, $K_a = V_a^{-1} + Z^\top W Z$ (prior covariance $V_a$, regressors $Z$, inverse variances $W$), had exact eigenvalues 14.8 and $1.9\times10^{17}$; forming $Z^\top W Z$ lost the small one and the Cholesky pivot was zero, although the conditional is benign, with standard deviations 0.20 and 0.16. % [R] commit 3d3f51b; NOTES 10 (60-digit arithmetic; cond 1.3e16)
The fix draws $a = R^{-1}(Q^\top\tilde r + z)$ from a Householder QR factorisation $[V_a^{-1/2};\, W^{1/2}Z] = QR$ with $\operatorname{diag}(R) > 0$, for the whitened right-hand side $\tilde r$ and injected noise $z$: $R$ is the upper Cholesky factor of $K_a$, so the noise map is unchanged, but $K_a$ is never formed; the factorisation works with the stacked matrix, whose condition number $\operatorname{cond}(K_a)^{1/2}$ is never squared. % [V] src/rai_bvar/engines/mcmc/linalg.py::lstsq_draw (docstring: "the condition number of A (√cond(K)) is never squared")
Tests cover agreement with the Cholesky draw to rounding, a synthetic extreme-scale state with $\operatorname{cond}(K_a) > 10^{15}$ checked against a 60-digit exact conditional, and a replay of chain 3214 in which the production step now succeeds. % [V] tests/mcmc/blocks/test_contemp_lstsq.py::test_lstsq_draw_equals_the_cholesky_draw (20 states x 5 compositions), ::test_extreme_scale_state (QR side only; the Cholesky path is not run there), ::test_g_full_reduced_sv_failing_chain_is_fixed (slow: old draw status 1003, production sweep finite)

\subsection[F2: the random-walk SV draw at a tiny sigma]{F2: the random-walk SV draw at $\sigma \approx 5\times10^{-9}$}\label{sec:robustness-f2}

The first full Clark--Ravazzolo attempt (code at 8a622c5) aborted with status 2001 (block \code{EQ_VOL}, equation~0) in an SV(rw) reduced-form fit at origin 1987Q2, burn-in sweep 318, with GDP's volatility scale at $\sigma = 4.98\times10^{-9}$. % [R] commit f60d736; NOTES 17 (ordering GDP, TBILL, INFL, UR; chain 0)
The state is valid: the non-centred prior $\sigma \sim N(0, B_\sigma)$, equivalently $\sigma^2 \sim B_\sigma\chi^2_1$, has positive density at $\sigma = 0$, and the same sweep's non-centred move would have returned $\sigma$ to 0.031. % [R] commit f60d736
In the scaled AWOL system $\tilde Q = \sigma^2 Q = \mathrm{Lap}_w + \operatorname{diag}(\delta)$, the terms $\delta_t = \sigma^2/v_{s_t} \approx 10^{-17}$ (mixture-component variances $v_{s_t}$, notation of \cref{sec:samp-rw}) fell below the floating-point spacing at the Laplacian's diagonal entries (2), so the assembled matrix was the singular path Laplacian and the level pivot, $\approx \sigma^2(1/V_0 + \sum_t 1/v_{s_t}) \approx 10^{-15}$, came out zero. % [R] commit f60d736 ("sigma^2 D ~ 1e-17", D = 1/v at observed t, test_sv_core.py l.305); DESIGN §3.7 (RW path)
The excess-pivot recursion of \cref{sec:samp-rw} factors the same matrix without assembling it, with non-negative terms only, so nothing cancels. % [V] src/rai_bvar/engines/mcmc/linalg.py::laplacian_precision_sample; tests/mcmc/blocks/test_sv_core.py::test_awol_rw_laplacian_is_the_rw_system
At a synthetic test state with $\sigma = 5\times10^{-9}$ and 88 periods, it reproduces the smallest pivot, $\log|\tilde Q|$ and $h$ of a 60-digit reference to relative errors of $10^{-12}$ or less on an Apple M5 Max. % [V] test_sv_core.py::test_rw_awol_at_a_tiny_sigma (problem(4, T_eff=88, T_pad=96, rw=True, sigma=5e-9): 88 periods, 2 of them masked by n_mask=2; pivot rel 1e-12, logdet rel 1e-13, h = sigma x rtol 1e-12), ::test_rw_step_at_a_tiny_sigma_is_ok

\subsection{SOC/SUR dummy rows on the MCMC engine}\label{sec:robustness-dummies}

In Mode~II the dummy rows are weighted by $1/\lambda_i$, so the $\lambda_i$ step must count them; a hand-injected omission was caught by the analytic twin (\cref{sec:validation-twins}) but not by the dense oracles of the coefficient step. % [R] NOTES 12 (the injection is not committed)
\todo{TODO 24: make the injection reproducible before quoting how many quantiles it moves.}
For exact Geweke and SBC references, $\lambda_i \mid d$ is inverse gamma under Mode~II, and the coefficients are then conditioned on the rows by Matheron's rule \citep{journel1978mining,chiles2012geostatistics,wilson2020efficiently}. % [V] NOTES 13; src/rai_bvar/validation/dummies.py docstring; tests/validation/test_reference_dummies.py::test_mode2_log_density_and_sampler
Under \code{iw_equivalent}, $p(d \mid \lambda) = N(r; 0, S(\lambda))$ with $S(\lambda) = S_0 + \lambda UU^\top$ is not of that form, but the base prior $\mathrm{IG}(c, C)$ is an exact rejection envelope: the acceptance probability $g(\lambda) = (|S_0|/|S(\lambda)|)^{1/2} \exp\{-\frac12(q(\lambda) - q_\infty)\}$, with $q(\lambda) = r^\top S(\lambda)^{-1} r$ and $q_\infty$ its limit as $\lambda \to \infty$, is at most one because $S(\lambda) \succeq S_0$ grows with $\lambda$ (\cref{app:derivations}). % [V] NOTES 13; dummies.py docstring (q_inf = GLS residual)
The $\lambda$ draws pass a Kolmogorov--Smirnov test against quadrature, and row 205 passes Geweke (maximum $|z|$ 2.24 in G-small, 2.76 in G-full) and SBC (minimum $p$ 0.018). % KS: [V] tests/validation/test_reference_dummies.py::test_iw_equivalent_rejection_sampler. Row 205 statistics (2.24, 2.76, 0.018): [R] NOTES 13 (no committed run record) + [REL] re-record on the release commit; row 205 = structural_iw_equivalent_dummies (src/rai_bvar/validation/joint.py)
The $O(q^2)$ eigen fast path survives the dense prior precision that the rows induce. % [V] tests/mcmc/blocks/test_dummies.py::test_dense_eigen_path_with_dummies_equals_cholesky_const_test_prior, ::test_dense_eigen_path_with_dummies_equals_cholesky_const_minnesota (timing App. E only)

\subsection{Notes for other implementations}\label{sec:robustness-notes}

The following generic checks describe no particular package.
(i)~Implementations that form a weighted Gram for a Gaussian conditional draw should check regressors whose scales differ by many orders of magnitude (F1).
(ii)~Implementations that solve the random-walk SV system in scaled form should check $\sigma \to 0$ (F2).
(iii)~Implementations of a non-centred sign fold should check that it flips $(\sigma, \tilde h)$ jointly, the map under which the posterior is invariant \citep{fruhwirth2010stochastic}, not $\sigma \mapsto |\sigma|$ alone (mutant M6, \cref{sec:validation-mutation}).
(iv)~Implementations that score multi-step forecasts conditionally on simulated paths should inspect the weight ESS for tail outcomes (\cref{sec:eval-tail}).
