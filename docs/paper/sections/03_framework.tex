\section{The model framework}\label{sec:framework}\label{sec:framework:notation}\label{sec:framework:delivered}

No model class of \pkg{rai-bvar} is new; each is credited where it is defined, and the package's
own choices within a class (the Mode~I scaling, the default SOC scope) are marked as such.
\cref{tab:components} lists the components by axis, the validation rows in which each appears,
and the roadmap. % [V] src/rai_bvar/validation/joint.py (_COMPOSITIONS); src/rai_bvar/components/specs.py
The data are $n$ variables $y_t$, observed at $t = 1,\dots,T$ after $p$ initial observations, with
regressors $x_t = (d_t', y_{t-1}', \dots, y_{t-p}')' \in \mathbb{R}^k$, $k = n_d + np$, where $d_t$
holds $n_d \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ deterministic terms (none, a constant, or a constant and a linear
trend). % [V] src/rai_bvar/data/design.py (module docstring, DETERMINISTIC_KINDS)
$Y$ (columns $y_i$) and $X$ stack the observations, $Y_{<i}$ holds the first $i-1$ columns of $Y$,
and the column order of the data is the recursive ordering. % [V] src/rai_bvar/data/dataset.py (module docstring)
$\kappa$ is the Minnesota overall tightness (code \code{lambda_}), $\iota$ (iota, not the index
$i$) the position of equation $i$ where it enters a prior formula as a number, $\lambda_{i,t}$ a
volatility, and $\mathrm{N}(m, V)$ has variance $V$; \cref{tab:TabA1} in \cref{app:notation}
lists the rest of the notation, with the code names. % [R] dossier 02 §2; DESIGN "Conventions". (TabA1 has no rows for soc/sur or the SV hyperpriors, hence "main".)

\subsection{The recursive structural form}\label{sec:framework:structural}

Every model estimated by MCMC is written as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:structural}
  A y_t = \Gamma x_t + \Lambda_t^{1/2}\varepsilon_t, \qquad
  \varepsilon_t \overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim} \mathrm{N}(0, I_n),
\end{equation}
with $A$ unit lower triangular (entries $a_{ij}$, $j < i$), $\Gamma$ with rows $\gamma_i'$, and
$\Lambda_t = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_{1,t},\dots,\lambda_{n,t})$. The reduced form is
$y_t = B'x_t + u_t$, $u_t \sim \mathrm{N}(0, \Sigma_t)$, with $B' = A^{-1}\Gamma$ and
$\Sigma_t = A^{-1}\Lambda_t A^{-1\prime}$. As $|A| = 1$, the log likelihood given the first $p$
observations is the reduced-form Gaussian log density with no Jacobian term,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:loglik}
  \log p(Y \mid A, \Gamma, \lambda)
    = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{t=1}^{T}\Big[-\tfrac12\log(2\pi\lambda_{i,t})
       - \frac{e_{i,t}^2}{2\lambda_{i,t}}\Big], \qquad
  e_{i,t} = y_{i,t} + {\textstyle\sum_{j<i}} a_{ij} y_{j,t} - x_t'\gamma_i .
\end{equation}
With priors independent across equations, equation $i$ is, given the volatilities, a
heteroskedastic regression with $k + i - 1$ regressors, so a sweep costs $O(nk^3) = O(n^4p^3)$
rather than the $O(n^6p^3)$ of a joint draw. % [R] SPEC §4.1; dossier 02 §3.4
This is exact in the structural form; a prior on the reduced-form $B$ couples the equations, and
there the package uses the corrected algorithm of \citet{carriero2022corrigendum}
(\cref{sec:samplers}). % [R] SPEC §0 table, §4.1; docs/phase2/04_cccm_corrected.md l.182 (Bognanni 2022 read from its abstract only; the corrigendum restates the argument)

\subsection{Priors}\label{sec:framework:priors}

All coefficient priors are of the Minnesota type \citep{litterman1986forecasting}, in a conjugate
form (analytic engine) or a structural or reduced form (MCMC engine; \cref{sec:architecture}
resolves \code{form="auto"}); \cref{app:prior-details} collects the options left out below.

\paragraph{Conjugate form.} The analytic engine uses the normal--inverse-Wishart (NIW) prior
\citep{kadiyala1997numerical,banbura2010large}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:omega}
  \Sigma \sim \mathrm{IW}(\operatorname{diag}\psi, d), \qquad
  \operatorname{vec} B \mid \Sigma \sim \mathrm{N}(\operatorname{vec} b, \Sigma \otimes \Omega), \qquad
  \Omega_{(l,j)} = \frac{\kappa^2 (d-n-1)}{l^{\alpha}\psi_j}, \qquad d = n + 2,
\end{equation}
with $\Omega$ diagonal, $(l,j)$ the coefficient on lag $l$ of variable $j$, $\alpha$ the lag decay
and $\Omega_{(d)} = \kappa_d$ for deterministic terms. The scales $\psi_j$ are residual variances of
univariate AR($p$) regressions on the estimation window, and $b$ is zero except on the own first
lag, where it is 1 for series in levels or log levels (transformation codes 1 and 4). % [V] src/rai_bvar/engines/analytic.py::_ar_residual_variances; src/rai_bvar/priors/minnesota.py (fields); src/rai_bvar/data/transforms.py (PERSISTENT_TCODES, prior_mean_own_lag)
The Kronecker structure ties own- and cross-lag tightness \citep{kadiyala1997numerical} and the
inverse Wishart fixes the contemporaneous prior (\cref{app:niw-structural}), so the structural
form's scales $\kappa_c$ (\code{cross}, on the prior standard deviation of other variables' lags)
and $\kappa_a$ (\code{contemp}, on the prior variance of $a_{ij}$) must be 1 here. % [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/minnesota.py (__post_init__); tests/unit/test_api_resolution.py::test_contemp_shrinkage_needs_a_non_conjugate_form; [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/structural.py (module docstring); DESIGN §6.1 (the two definitions, carried from the old Mode I sentence: kappa_c scales a prior s.d., kappa_a a prior variance)
The prior stays conjugate with sum-of-coefficients (SOC) and single-unit-root (SUR) dummy rows
\citep{doan1984forecasting,sims1998bayesian}, built as in \citet{giannone2015prior} except that by
default (\code{soc_scope="persistent"}, our convention) SOC rows exist only for variables with a
non-zero own-lag prior mean; % [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/dummies.py (Rows); src/rai_bvar/engines/analytic.py (Conventions)
with the hierarchy of \citet{giannone2015prior} for the hyperparameters $\vartheta$
(\code{hierarchy="fixed"}, \code{"eb"}, the default, or \code{"mh"}); % NOTATION: the hyperparameter vector is written \vartheta, not h (h_{i,t} is the log-volatility and h the horizon, App. A); assembler: add \vartheta to TabA1. [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/hyperpriors.py (GLPHyperpriors); src/rai_bvar/engines/analytic.py (module docstring)
and with the COVID-19 scaling of \citet{lenza2022estimate}, which divides the rows of $Y$ and $X$
by a common scale $s_t$, equal to 1 before a start period $t^*$ (by default the period containing
March 2020). % [V] src/rai_bvar/data/outliers.py

\paragraph{Structural form.} This default of the MCMC engine centres the coefficients
conditionally on the contemporaneous row,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:centring}
  \mathrm{E}[\gamma_i \mid a_i] = M\tilde a_i, \qquad
  \tilde a_i = (a_{i1}, \dots, a_{i,i-1}, 1, 0, \dots, 0)',
\end{equation}
with $M$ the reduced-form prior mean $b$, so that $\mathrm{E}[B' \mid A] = M'$ for every $A$ (D3 in
the design documents; \cref{app:niw-structural}). With $M$ the random-walk mean (identity on the
first lag, zeros elsewhere), this is the conditional centring of the structural prior of
\citet{sims1998bayesian}, who normalise the structural shocks to unit variance and leave the
diagonal of $A$ free; a linear centring is unchanged when $a_i$ and $\gamma_i$ are rescaled
together. \citet{sims2006were} write it, in their notation, as $A_+ = D + SA_0$ with a zero-mean
prior on $D$ (in ours, column $i$ of $D$ is $\gamma_i - M\tilde a_i$ up to the scale of the row).

Sims & Zha, FRB Atlanta WP 96-13 (Oct. 1996; WP version of the IER article per IDEAS, https://ideas.repec.org/p/fip/fedawp/96-13.html): unit-variance shocks in the likelihood, eqs. Mar. 27, 2005), sec. III, eq. (7), p. 9 (https://gceps.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/110sims.pdf); AER numbering not seen. S&Z's zero-mean D is not our delta_i (which has mean m_i)
In the coordinates $\delta_i = \gamma_i - M_{<i}a_i$ ($M_{<i}$ the first $i-1$ columns of $M$;
unit Jacobian), $a_i$ and $\delta_i$ are independent a priori with diagonal precisions
(\cref{alg:C-structural}). % [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/structural.py (module docstring)

Three prior modes share this form (\cref{app:niw-structural,app:mode1}; tests in
\cref{app:prior-tests}). \emph{Mode~I}, the package's own design and its default, used by
\code{Constant("ig")} and every SV arm, puts the fixed scale $\bar\sigma_i^2 = \psi_i/\iota$ in place of $\lambda_i$ in the coefficient
prior variances. % [R] docs/phase2/DESIGN.md D-10; docs/phase2/05_structural_equation_sampler.md §0 item 5; [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/structural.py (module docstring); DESIGN §6.1
At $\kappa_c = \kappa_a = 1$ it matches exactly the reduced-form mean (for every $A$), the marginal
coefficient variances and $\mathrm{E}\,\Sigma = \operatorname{diag}\psi$ of the NIW prior, but not
its distribution, and avoids the growth $\mathrm{E}\,\Sigma_{ii} = 2^{i-1}\psi_i$ along the ordering
\citep{chan2024large} of the unscaled choice. % [V] tests/priors/test_structural_prior.py::test_mode_i_matches_the_niw_moments_exactly_and_by_monte_carlo; tests/integration/test_ordering.py::test_prior_mode_reproduces_the_cky_two_to_the_i_growth (psi = 1); [R] general psi: docs/phase2/05_structural_equation_sampler.md §0 item 5
\emph{Mode~II} (\code{Constant("iw")} with a structural mean) scales both blocks by $\lambda_i$
instead; at $\kappa_c = \kappa_a = 1$ it is exactly the structural form of the NIW prior \citep[Corollary~1]{chan2022asymmetric}, so its posterior is the
NIW posterior, % [R] docs/phase2/05_structural_equation_sampler.md §4.2 (proposition, proof sketch; l.199 "Cor. 1 ... Prop. 1 is the converse"), §1 table (Chan's Prop. 1 and Cor. 1-3 cross-checked against the QE PDF), §13 item 1 / l.1036 (citation corrected to Cor. 1); [V] tests/priors/test_structural_prior.py::test_mode_ii_prior_is_the_niw_prior; tests/analytic/test_structural_nig.py::test_a1_log_ml_equals_phase1
and with $\kappa_c \neq 1$ it is the asymmetric conjugate prior of \citet{chan2022asymmetric} with
the centring \eqref{eq:centring}. % [R] src/rai_bvar/engines/structural_nig.py (module docstring); twin A4, tests/analytic/test_mcmc_twin.py::test_a4_chan_asymmetric_prior (nightly)
\code{Constant("iw_equivalent")} scales only $a_i$ by $\lambda_i$; at $\kappa_a = 1$ the implied
$\Sigma$ is exactly $\mathrm{IW}(\operatorname{diag}\psi, n+2)$
\citep[Proposition~1]{chan2022asymmetric}, and in reduced form the model is invariant to the
ordering of the variables. % [V] tests/priors/test_structural_prior.py::test_iw_equivalent_sigma_is_inverse_wishart; tests/integration/test_ordering.py::test_reduced_iw_equivalent_is_order_invariant_within_mcse (slow tier; not re-run for the dossier)

\paragraph{Reduced form.} The columns $\pi_j$ of $B$ get independent priors
$\mathrm{N}(m_j, \psi_j\Omega^{(j)})$, with $m_j$ column $j$ of $M$ and $\Omega^{(j)}$ the $\Omega$ of
\eqref{eq:omega} with the entries of other variables' lags multiplied by $\kappa_c^2$, which match
the NIW marginal variances at $\kappa_c = 1$; the prior on $(A, \lambda)$ is the structural one. % [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/reduced.py (module docstring); Omega^(j) definition: src/rai_bvar/priors/structural.py (module docstring); DESIGN §6.1

\paragraph{Hyperparameters on the MCMC engine.} These are fixed, by default at the empirical-Bayes
mode of the analytic conjugate twin fitted at $\kappa_c = \kappa_a = 1$ on the same window, so that
every volatility arm in a comparison shares the mean prior; except in Mode~II this is a documented
plug-in, not the estimated model's own mode (\cref{app:prior-details}). % [V] tests/analytic/test_eb_mcmc.py::test_eb_plug_in_equals_the_phase1_eb_mode_at_cross_1; src/rai_bvar/engines/mcmc/eb.py (module docstring, item 1: "a documented plug-in for Mode I, the reduced form and SV"); [R] DESIGN §6.5

\subsection{Volatility arms}\label{sec:framework:vol}

\paragraph{Constant.} Under \code{Constant}, $\lambda_{i,t} = \lambda_i \sim \mathrm{IG}(1 + \iota/2,
\psi_i/2)$, with mean $\bar\sigma_i^2 = \psi_i/\iota$, in all three modes. % [V] src/rai_bvar/components/vol/constant.py (module docstring)

\paragraph{Stochastic volatility.} Under \code{SV} the log-variance $h_{i,t} = \log\lambda_{i,t}$
of each structural equation follows
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sv}
  h_{i,t} = \mu_i + \phi_i(h_{i,t-1} - \mu_i) + \sigma_i\eta_{i,t}, \qquad
  \eta_{i,t} \overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim} \mathrm{N}(0, 1),
\end{equation}
or, with \code{rw=True}, the random walk $h_{i,t} = h_{i,t-1} + \sigma_i\eta_{i,t}$. The package's
default priors, in the parametrisation of \pkg{stochvol}
\citep{kastner2016dealing,hosszejni2021modeling}, are $\mu_i \sim \mathrm{N}(\log(\psi_i/\iota), 10)$,
centred in each window on the log of the Mode~I scale; $(\phi_i + 1)/2 \sim \mathrm{Beta}(20, 1.5)$; $\sigma_i^2 \sim
B_\sigma\chi^2_1$ with $B_\sigma = 1$ ($0.1$ for the random walk), or an inverse gamma; $h_{i,0}$ from
the stationary law, or $\mathrm{N}(\log(\psi_i/\iota), 4)$ for the random walk. % [V] src/rai_bvar/components/specs.py (SV fields, _PHI_PRIOR, _default_sigma2); src/rai_bvar/priors/sv_arrays.py::sv_prior_arrays
The replication's primary arm (\cref{sec:replication}), reduced form $\times$ \code{SV(rw=True)}, is
validation row 18; its Student-$t$ variant has no row of its own
(\cref{sec:validation-coverage}). % [V] at 7f410dc: src/rai_bvar/validation/joint.py _COMPOSITIONS ("reduced_sv_rw": _Def(18, _R, rw=True, n=(3, 3)); rows 1-18 and 201-205, 23 in all; rw only in rows 14 and 18); benchmarks/clark_ravazzolo.py::models (reduced SV(rw), SV(rw,t10)), PRIMARY_SV = "SV(rw)". The factorisation $\Sigma_t = A^{-1}\Lambda_t A^{-1\prime}$ with a constant unit lower-triangular
$A$ and independent univariate log-variances is the volatility model of \citet{cogley2005drifts},
who combine it with drifting coefficients and random-walk log-variances; \citet{primiceri2005time}
uses the same triangular factorisation but lets $A$ drift and leaves the covariance of the
log-volatility innovations unrestricted. With constant coefficients and \code{rw=True}, the
volatility specification is that of the BVAR with stochastic volatility of
\citet{clark2011realtime} (constant $A$, independent random-walk log-variances), who takes it from
\citet{cogley2005drifts} and writes the VAR in mean-adjusted form with a steady-state prior.

\paragraph{Robust SV.} Outliers and Student-$t$ errors are transitory scales of the structural
residual,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:robust}
  e_{i,t} = o_{i,t}\,\tau_{i,t}^{1/2}\,e^{h_{i,t}/2}\,\varepsilon_{i,t}, \qquad
  \lambda_{i,t} = e^{h_{i,t}}\,o_{i,t}^2\,\tau_{i,t},
\end{equation}
and $\lambda_{i,t}$ in \eqref{eq:loglik} is then this effective variance. With \code{outliers=True},
as in \citet{carriero2024addressing}, $o_{i,t} \in \{1, \dots, 20\}$ is 1 with probability $1 - p_i$
and otherwise uniform on $\{2, \dots, 20\}$, with a Beta prior on $p_i$. % [V] src/rai_bvar/priors/sv_arrays.py::outlier_beta; src/rai_bvar/components/specs.py (outlier_max=20, _default_outlier); src/rai_bvar/components/vol/transitory.py (w_k = p/(K-1)); [R] docs/phase2/03_sv_outliers_t.md §0 table (CCMM REStat §III.A "U(2,20)", online appendix II.iv fn. 8, code grid 1:20)
The two-part outlier distribution and the Beta prior on $p_i$ follow the unobserved-components
model of \citet{stock2016core}, whose outlier scale is uniform on $[2, 10]$; the discrete grid
follows \citet{carriero2024addressing}, who describe it as a tractable approximation.
