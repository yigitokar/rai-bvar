\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
% Drafting notes (not typeset): the only outcome-dependent statement is the \acc{} slot in the first

A model comparison is only as credible as its weakest link: the sampler, convergence at every
origin, the Monte Carlo error of the score, the variable ordering, the validity of the test, and
choices made after the scores were seen. \pkg{rai-bvar} makes each of these links checkable, and
the preceding sections show the evidence for each. The econometrics is not new; the contribution is
the integration, the contracts between the components, and the evidence, with its measured power
and its stated gaps, that the samplers target the intended posteriors. For an applied forecaster this means that a BVAR is composed from a
mean, a volatility and a prior component, fitted by whichever engine the composition requires,
scored through one predictive contract, and compared by a verdict engine that credits a gain only
when the two models differ along exactly one axis, so that a volatility gain is never credited to
the mean or the prior, and that withholds a verdict which the Monte Carlo error or the convergence
of the fits cannot support. The validation code behind that evidence ships with the library. % [V] src/rai_bvar/validation/ (part of the package; dossier 01 §2, commitment 4)

\paragraph{A checklist.}
The lessons of building and validating the package, for forecasters and for implementers of the
same algorithms, are these.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Write every kernel as a pure function of injected noise, and expose each exact draw's
    conditional and each Metropolis--Hastings step's log acceptance ratio
    (\cref{sec:arch-mcmc}).
  \item Write the reference prior samplers, simulators and joint densities independently of the
    kernels, and enforce the separation with an import test (\cref{sec:validation-harness}).
    % [V] tests/validation/test_log_joint.py::test_references_never_import_kernels
  \item Check an identity at a joint draw for every conditional and every Metropolis--Hastings step;
    run prior-started Geweke chains and SBC with data-dependent test quantities, including the exact
    log-likelihood (\cref{sec:validation}).
  \item Measure the power of the suite with injected bugs, among them errors of published
    samplers, and degrade an approximation on purpose to test its exact correction
    (\cref{sec:validation-mutation}).
    % [V] tests/mutation/mutants.py: M1 (CCM-2019 triangular step), M2 (Primiceri 2005 Alg. 1 indicator order); M7 with the COARSE1 table
  \item Treat numerical failures as values: never add jitter, and fix the kernel rather than the
    test prior (\cref{sec:robustness}).
  \item Check convergence at every origin and gate verdicts on it, and size the MCMC budget with fit-only
    diagnostics before any score exists (\cref{sec:eval-verdict,sec:replication-prereg}).
  \item Do not read the delta-method MCSE of a Rao--Blackwellised log score as a bound on its tail
    error; inspect the effective number of weights \citep{kish1965survey}, and at $h > 1$ integrate
    the simulated path out and use enough forecast draws (\cref{sec:eval-tail}).
  \item Report RB and Gaussian-approximation scores side by side, the ordering spread next to the
    gain, and an order-invariant negative control (\cref{sec:replication-design,sec:replication-results}).
  \item Treat Diebold--Mariano $p$-values under expanding windows as descriptive, and state which
    route produced each verdict (\cref{sec:eval-tests}).
  \item Pre-register settings as code, say how blind the pre-registration is, and make the absence
    of look-ahead a run-time check (\cref{sec:replication-data,sec:replication-prereg}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Roadmap and availability.}
The roadmap continues by phase: global-local priors, common stochastic volatility and conditional
forecasts; non-linear means; time-varying parameters, regime switching and factor or
order-invariant volatility; and identification beyond the recursive scheme. % [R] SPEC.md §13
We make no promise about results. The next priority is the size and power study of the verdict
engine on simulated data (\cref{sec:limitations}), followed by the Phase~3 prior axis, so
that a two-axis ablation can be shown. \pkg{rai-bvar} is licensed under Apache-2.0.
